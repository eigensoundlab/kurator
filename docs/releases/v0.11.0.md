# Kurator 0.11.0 – waveform view, sound analysis, look filters, timeline, sprite sheets and atlases

A release for sound and game work. Sounds get a proper waveform view and are measured. Pictures can
be filtered by how they look, and long libraries get a timeline. Selections become sprite sheets and
texture atlases a game engine can read.

## Highlights

### Waveform view with a playhead
- Clicking a sound shows its waveform with a **playhead** in the side panel. Enter (or double-click)
  opens it full size with a time ruler.
- Click to play from any point and drag to scrub. The wheel zooms into a part; double-click shows the
  whole sound again. Space plays and pauses.
- The menu in the corner switches the style:
  - **Peak + RMS**
  - **Min / max** with clipped samples in red
  - **Level (dB)** from −60 to 0 dBFS
  - **Stereo lanes**
  - **Spectrogram**
- More styles will come later. Your choice is remembered.

### Loudness, tempo and key
- Every sound gets its integrated **loudness in LUFS** (ITU-R BS.1770 / EBU R128) and its loudest
  moment.
- Sounds of 8 s and longer also get an estimated **tempo (BPM)** and **key**. The panel marks both
  as estimated.
- Sort by *Length*, *Tempo* or *Loudness*. Filter by tempo, key and length in **Look ▾**.

### Look ▾ filter
- **Colours:** 12 hues plus black, gray and white. A picture matches when a colour covers a good part
  of it.
- **Shape:** landscape, portrait, square, panorama, tall.
- **Minimum size**, and **has transparency**.
- **Sounds:** tempo, key and length.
- The side panel shows a picture's main colours; click one to copy its hex code.
- Look filters appear as a chip in the path bar and are kept in saved views.

### Timeline
- When the grid is sorted by date, a timeline sits on its right edge. It marks years and months, with
  a bar showing how many items each month holds.
- Hover to see the month, click to jump to it, drag to move through the years.

### Sprite sheets and texture atlases
- **J → Sprites / atlas** offers two kinds:
  - **Texture atlas:** images packed tightly, with transparent borders trimmed. You can set padding,
    edge extrusion (no bleeding with filtering), a power-of-two size, a maximum size and a scale.
  - **Sprite sheet:** equal cells for animation frames. Animated GIF, WebP and APNG files are split
    into their frames.
- Both save a **PNG plus a JSON** file in the TexturePacker *JSON Hash* layout, which Phaser, PixiJS,
  Godot and Unity importers read. The JSON holds frame rectangles, trim offsets, source sizes and
  `animations`.

## Smaller changes
- The toolbar wraps onto a second line on narrower windows instead of pushing the side panel off the
  screen.
- The side panel no longer shows the previous sound's waveform when nothing is focused.
- Join, contact sheet and atlas refuse an empty *Save into* field. Before, they saved into the folder
  Kurator was started from.

## Upgrading
- Run `update.bat` / `./update.sh`. The catalog is migrated automatically.
- **On the first start, existing sound files are analysed once** for loudness, tempo and key. Their
  previews are made again in the background, so the grid stays usable meanwhile; large sound
  libraries take a few minutes.
- About 20 seconds after the start, colours for existing pictures are filled in from the thumbnails
  in the background.
