# Kurator 0.14.0 – print views, text-to-sound search, Unity import settings, KTX2

## Highlights

### Print and desktop publishing
- **Colour-managed previews.** Pictures with an embedded ICC profile (CMYK print files, Adobe RGB,
  ProPhoto, Gray) are converted to sRGB through that profile. They look as they print or as
  Photoshop shows them, not over-saturated or washed out.
- **Side panel:**
  - colour mode, profile and bit depth;
  - print size at the picture's own resolution and at 300 ppi;
  - for PDF and Illustrator files: page size (trim box), bleed, PDF version, PDF/X conformance,
    output intent, spot colours (e.g. *PANTONE 185 C*) and fonts (and whether they are subsets).
- **◧ Inks** in the viewer (**I**), for CMYK pictures:
  - the **C, M, Y, K plates**, in their ink colour or grey;
  - **total ink** with an adjustable **ink limit**: areas above it turn red. Typical limits are
    300 % for coated paper, 260–280 % uncoated, 240 % newsprint;
  - the ink percentages under the mouse.
- **Soft proofing for RGB pictures.** Choose a CMYK profile for your printing condition in
  ⚙ Settings → Print. Profiles installed by Adobe apps, ECI/FOGRA or printer drivers are listed, or
  give a path. ◧ Inks then shows:
  - the *Proof* (how the picture prints);
  - the plates as the picture would be separated;
  - the total ink;
  - **Gamut**: green marks colours the press can't reach.
- **▭ Boxes** draws the trim and bleed boxes over a PDF page in the viewer.

### Text-to-sound search
- With the AI add-on, sounds get a fingerprint from **LAION CLAP**, as pictures do from SigLIP 2.
- `?rain on a tin roof`, `?wooden door creak` or `?sad solo piano` in the search box find matching
  sounds. Matching pictures come in the same list.
- **S** on a sound finds sounds that sound alike.
- AI ▸ *Prepare images and sounds* computes them. You can choose a general model, a music and
  speech model, or switch sounds off.

### Unity sprite import settings
- For pictures inside a Unity project, the side panel has *Unity import…*. It writes the `.meta`
  files the way the Inspector would:
  - texture type Sprite;
  - pixels per unit;
  - filter (Point for pixel art);
  - compression and mip maps;
  - the picture's `_normal` / `_mask` maps linked as secondary textures for 2D lights.
- Existing `.meta` files keep their GUID and all other settings. **Undo** restores the old files.
- Sprite sheets and atlases saved into a Unity project get a `.meta` with the sprites already
  sliced (Sprite Mode *Multiple*). Trimmed sprites keep their original pivot.

### KTX2 textures
- KTX2 files are now indexed and previewed:
  - uncompressed 8-bit and HDR float formats;
  - BC1–BC7;
  - ETC2/EAC and ASTC, with the 3D add-on;
  - Zstd or zlib supercompression.
- Basis Universal content (ETC1S/UASTC) is listed with its facts and a card. Its transcoder has no
  Python package with wheels for every platform yet.

## Fixes
- The side panel could fail on Blender files with *TypeError: 'int' object is not iterable*.
- The preview in the side panel no longer grows or shrinks when the sharper picture arrives or the
  scrollbar appears. The picture gets its final size at once.
- A test failed on macOS because of case-insensitive disks.

## New dependencies
- AI add-on: `transformers` (for CLAP).
- 3D add-on: `texture2ddecoder` (ETC2/ASTC).
- pip-audit reports no known vulnerabilities in either environment.

## Upgrading
- Run `update.bat` / `./update.sh`. If you use the AI features, the update installs `transformers`.
- Then run AI ▸ *Prepare images and sounds* once. The CLAP model (about 0.8 GB) downloads on first
  use.
